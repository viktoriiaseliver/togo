Togo is a shared dog journal for the whole family, on iPhone.
Who took the dog out? Has anyone fed them? Check the journal and pick up where the last person left off.

Less checking in. More knowing.
Log an outing, a meal, a bowel movement or a medication dose. Everyone you invite can see the day’s entries, so caring for your dog doesn’t depend on catching each other at the door. If two people log the same thing, Togo offers to merge the entries instead of quietly overwriting one.

Get to know your dog’s routine
Your dog is asking to go out. Is it time? Togo uses the outings you log to help you spot their usual rhythm. When there isn’t enough information, it says so. You can always choose to take your dog out.
You can also note how your dog asked. If barking starts taking the place of their usual quiet signal, Togo helps you notice the change.

Keep track of care
Medication at a set time. A period without food before a dose. Togo reminds you of the treatment instructions you’ve recorded and helps you avoid giving the same dose twice. Your notes can also become a report for the vet, without starting from a blank page. You choose who can view health information.
Time-sensitive alerts cover medication, missed doses and periods without food. Grooming, vaccinations and weigh-ins come together in one reminder at the time you choose.
Togo keeps records and sends reminders. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or replace your vet.

Pack for your dog, too
Travel mode starts and ends on your planned dates. Togo calculates how much food to pack and helps you plan feeding times before a flight.

Your journal, your space
There’s no Togo account to create. Your journal stays on your iPhone and, if iCloud is enabled, in your private iCloud storage. Family sharing uses iCloud and is by invitation only. Togo has no server storing your journal.

Free for one dog and two people
All everyday features are included. Help avoiding duplicate doses, reminders about periods without food, notes on how your dog asks to go out, and the reminder not to punish an indoor accident stay free, with no time limit.
Togo Pro adds more dogs and lets you share with more than two people. Everything else is free.

Togo Pro is a monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription. The price is shown in the App Store. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm your purchase. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. On your iPhone, go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Togo > Cancel Subscription.
