Togo — a dog diary for the whole family. Who took her out, who fed her, what the stool looked like — it's written down, and nobody has to work it out in the evening.

THE MAIN BUTTON — “NEEDS TO GO OUT?”
In three or four days the app learns your dog's intervals and tells you whether the need is real. While it has few entries it says “not sure” — honestly — and still puts “Taking her out” first. It doesn't know better than you do.

ONE TAP THAT CHANGES BEHAVIOR
The door opens, and whatever the dog did just before it gets reinforced. Togo asks how exactly she asked, and shows you if barking is crowding out the quiet signal.

A SHARED DIARY
Whoever you invite sees the day's entries and dose marks: outings, meals, stools, “needs to go out”. Two entries about the same thing don't overwrite each other — the app offers to merge them. No working out in the evening who took her out.

MEDICINE IS A MODE, NOT A SECTION
The Health tab is open to whoever you trust with medical records: a helper can be left without it. Fasting window, double-dose protection, the dose → blood draw protocol, and a report for the vet — assembled from what's already written down.

TRIPS
The mode switches on by date and goes out by itself. Packing is calculated from weight and portion size, pre-flight fasting windows from the departure time.

WHAT INTERRUPTS YOU
Only a dose on the clock, a fasting window, and a missed dose. Grooming, vaccines and weigh-ins arrive in a single message at the hour you choose.

FREE FOREVER
The fasting window, double-dose protection, “how she asked”, “didn't take food”, and the warning about punishing a dog after an accident. Without them the app would quietly harm the dog, so they aren't for sale.

TOGO PRO
A second dog and more, a family larger than two people. Everything else — along with the fasting window and double-dose protection — is free forever.
Togo Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly. The App Store shows the price: it depends on your country and can change. The subscription renews itself until you cancel — no later than 24 hours before the period ends. Payment is charged to your Apple ID on purchase confirmation. You can cancel in App Store settings.

FIVE LANGUAGES
English, Русский, Français, Українська, Қазақша. You switch the language in settings — no need to restart the phone.

We have no server of our own, and there's no account to create. The diary lives on your phone; if iCloud is on, a copy goes to your private library, in the same place as everything else of yours. The shared family diary is iCloud sharing: only the person you name yourself gets in.
